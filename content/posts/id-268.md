How are you supposed to keep calm when you are surrounded by demons?

I guess that's where the merit is. We need to make discomfort our expertise field. Otherwise we would not be good soldiers. Ready for everything. After all, how can we fully enjoy peace if we never experienced chaos?

Still there's some discomfort we can avoid. Sometimes the problem is us, rather than the problem itself. Of course that's too much for us to accept.

There's a moment in Ayahuasca. Specially recognizable when done the right way. A moment of inaction. Everything stops and calms down. That's your soul feeling the rope, the rope of the soul.

It grabs you so strongly that you feel comfy, safe. Like a child ready to explore the world on a piggyback ride.

This is a moment for you to enjoy, to not think in anything else, just feeling the medicine all over the place. A calmness that you never experienced before, and the one you'll want to come back.

But for this you need to be safe and comfortable as when you sit on your couch. There can't be any distraction, anything interrupting the connection between you and mother earth. That's why you came, right?

You don't have to know any more stories. You don't have to look for more. You don't have to choose between status and sympathy. There's only one amazon rainforest. That's luckily where the source still holds. Even enduring attacks of time, humans and shows they put up.
