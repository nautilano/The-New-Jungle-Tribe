No longer you take things for granted. No longer you believe in randomness.

Now you are awake, and now the truth you can handle.

It will come to you and it may hurt. But it won't be rejected. No longer running in circles. No longer small bits of it entering.

You get it all now. And words and days will be short.

Life is a gift. A blessing. A small moment of the whole. You are part of it. Without you there's nothing.

So you do it for you, you do it for us."
