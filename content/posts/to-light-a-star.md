"I can light a star. But that star won't fall for you. You may need to follow her all your life. Always loving unconditionally.

You may not build her house, but you may make her world a better place. That's pure love and that's what you signed for. Every other thing out there are just concepts made out from humans. I didn't create those. I made love. A whole, pure and magic accident. It doesn't make any sense. Because it doesn't depend on anything.

It's the number one thing in life to never find a reason for. From the beginning to the end.

You shall live and die in the name of love because that's the sacred duty coded in the creation, from where you came and where you go. A magic, incomprehensible strong force whose only task is to keep everything together. After that there's nothing else. Nothing to celebrate, nothing to die for.

You see the importance of your task? Be proud and be grateful. You get to love like never before. The same feeling, the same sensation. You'll feel it on your bones, all the love, all the light, the same power of creation.

Yes, to see the star you may have to open the right doors, but if you are lucky those will never close."
