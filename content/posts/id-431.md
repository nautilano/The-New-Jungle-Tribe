“I don’t think this story will be ever over. At least I don’t think I’ll be conscious when it happens.

Yes, maybe Gaia will be there to see. Luckily I won’t be there to receive the punishment.

That of course If me and my other lives are meant to fail.

Would that be possible? To fail one and one more time trying to get in your heart.

I thought in this life it would be it. I thought maybe after all my experiences this would be the one for me to win.

But apparently there is no win and losses in this game. There’s only playing and trying.

Just you my darling. Only you have the answer.

Only you can make everything happen, and everything not to happen”
