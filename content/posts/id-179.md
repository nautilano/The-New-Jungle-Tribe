There was an accident in a lab because it had to happen. Probably one of the most needed accidents of them all.

A proof and a sign of the powerful attraction of the source. We only get slowed down, we don't run away from it. The bullet has already been shot. We are just experiencing the journey to the target.

You only are conscious of it when you wake up. This is the tricky part of this "dream" stuff. No one can tell but yourself. So stop blindly believing in what others are saying, because they are also living a multiple dream.

So yes, if you have the chance to try psychedelics in a responsible way, do it. Waking up can be done through experiences that shake our already "known" reality that has being built for many years. Yes, meditation, yoga and other less intense alternatives can also help the light get in, but they are not reality destroyers. We need strong experiences to break the giant roof of ego, of fake comfort that has been built on top of us and is preventig us to fly, to get information from the sun.

But how? How to decide where to start? Lucky for us. There's a teacher in the school of universe that has got our back.

This teacher is a mistery. He doesn't even go to classes often. We don't even know if its a he, she, or it. Yet, somehow, he finds a way to deliver the right information to the students that need the most. He knows of tricks, shortcuts, and magic skills. He's the one who will call his friends and make you a visit because you believe in aliens.

 This teacher is actually considered "the weirdo" by the others, but is still loved, respected and even feared by the ones who know its true power. The same power that made LSD appear. The same power that is making you read this book. His ways are unknown, but always work. Some say it's Gaia's attempt to fix it all.

Breaking barriers of unknown laws, and coming through different layers of realms. It comes to you to take you out of the boring class, and take you to a trip where you don't want to come back. The kind of trip you face death itself and you realize there's nothing to be afraid.
