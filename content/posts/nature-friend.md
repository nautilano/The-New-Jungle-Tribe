"You may have not noticed. But I do. Life has granted me the experience and blessed my eyes with full capacity of appreciation.

I speak from experience and understanding, so this is not just me being corny. Though for you I would be that with no problem.

I see you, I see how the light touches your face and the wind blows when you smile. I see how the trees all shake for you to notice, for you to go and hug them. They all dance and the birds sing. They celebrate your presence, they are all so happy and partying. For a moment nothing matters, just you, Gaia herself in her garden.

Don't you notice? I guess this is just for the fun, because she's just playful all the time. You are her, you are the creation herself. The beginning and continuation. The one trigger that makes everything be and my heart bump until it can't keep. So much love, and so much beauty. It's really hard not to surrender and see.”
