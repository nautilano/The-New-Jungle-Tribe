“Show yourself. It’s alright, you are safe.

Darling. I can see who you really are. I’m sorry I can’t be anyone else.

But maybe one day I’ll be enough at the right time. And then at that moment you’ll be free and we’ll both enjoy life the way it’s supposed to be.

One day though, if I don’t get to see, I know you’ll be shining the most.

Looking the most beautiful. The most healthy. The most perfect bride for any man.

That’s all I really want. And I’ll live and die happily. Knowing that flower I saw from far away is now blooming in eternal beauty.

And then I will realize I was actually lucky, for being able to see that flower exist.

Nothing can take that from me. The joy, the awareness. The passion I feel. I guess that was it for me. And that’s more than I could ever ask in a thousand lives”
