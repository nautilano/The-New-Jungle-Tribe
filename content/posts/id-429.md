Don't always connect things to your own world, because it's small. No matter how big you think it is. We are small in the grand scheme of things.

Believe me. When something is already relevant for you to even care, you will notice. Already the action of ignoring is a sign that your energy is expanding it’s playing field.

So yes, there’s a moment that your dream is so joyful that everything else is happening on the background, and you are just fine not even knowing about it.

But when it happens. Because it will happen at some point. The dream will be over and you’ll see yourself more aware of things that you weren’t prepared before.

That’s alright. It happens to all of us but you shall be strong. There’s no other option but being strong.

On the practice you will see yourself handling better the crazyness of the world that now is trying to bring you in.

Relax and don’t lose yourself in the madness. Sometimes a change of environment is the only answer. But for that you need to learn to walk on fire.
