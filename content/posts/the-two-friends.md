"You can't, they can only believe what their eyes can see. So for now be proud of what you got. Your soul knows and that’s enough.

That's what the medicine is for, so you need to find a way to protect it and when the time comes, bring them back to the source.

Yes, it will be hard, cause for them to leave their egos behind, it may take them so many lives. And maybe time will pass and their souls will be so dark, so clouded, that they won't really have a chance to get back.

Worry no more. This is something you need to accept. Only them can be saved on their own.

Your job is very clear. Save, protect and free the medicine when the time comes”
