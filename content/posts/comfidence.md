If only the Yacuruna would have heard….If only he knew there were some hunters close by, spying their conversation. Maybe the little warrior could have enjoyed more of his jungle life. I guess The Yacuruna didn't have super sensitive abilities as their enemies used to believe. One more proof that humans speak bullshit since the beginning of times.

The truth is that Yacurunas had different abilities that goes beyond our comprehension. And their relationship with humankind and the planet is way deeper that we can imagine. You see, their power was focused in a more subtle way. They had a sacred trusting power. A blind confidence of good outcomes. Since the doors of the source were never really closed to them.

They had assistance and support everytime they needed. More than a blessing, they worked for it. They knew, even if time gets many lives on the way, justice will come and they will celebrate. Yes, a smile always, even in the darkest times. No enemies can beat that. They can take their lives but their light will always shine. The same light that holds all of their kind. The same light Gaia works so hard to keep alive.

You see, they understand their battle is bigger and more important than what their current awareness can say. So they will fight for it until their last breath. So when The Yacuruna found out the little warrior was beaten to death, he was not so devastated. Even after having deep talks and developing a sincere friendship. He knew there was a big reason behind all of this.

He was right. Gaia knew all along. She knew exactly what she was doing, and she made sure the little warrior gets some love after.
