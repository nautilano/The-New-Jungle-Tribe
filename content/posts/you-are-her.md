"She has a natural comfy vibe that only comes with truth.

You see her, and more importantly, she sees you.

See her beauty and surrend to her. Before any hug or touch, just look at her.

Look until no sound is heard and the only bump you feel is your heart.

She is everywhere. She is inside. You can feel her energy flowing, her divinity unfolding. It covers and protects, everything suddenly makes sense.

Nothing to explain, nothing to say. If you feel, that's it, you embrace the feeling.

Now hug if she wants, you are on your lucky day."
