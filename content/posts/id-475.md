“Where’s your hunger? Dear warrior?

Where’s your passion? The one you always praise about

What about the unstoppable force of will that you have inside? Is it gone? Disappeared? Consumed?

You had it all. Literally everything a man can ever want. And you lost it.

So what’s preventing you now from going to war?

I’m not saying you’ll die in battle. You think I would let that happen? After everything I did for you?

Well. At least you lived and you enjoyed. Most of men never get to that.

So either you man up and look for redemption now, or you will succumb to the worst shadows of your soul.

Again. This is your choice. It has always been like that. You were just so busy crying about your losses. What about the wins? What about the man you are now?

That’s no coincidence. It’s been always you. Driving on the manhood path. You were just not ready for how long it was

Redemption or death. Either way you’ll be in heaven again”
