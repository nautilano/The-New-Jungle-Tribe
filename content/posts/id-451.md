If you have read many stories you would understand that the Ayahuasca environment is just one of them. It’s not different than any other. That’s the truth we need to accept.

Yes, in Ayahuasca there is a bad and there is a good. Simpler than any retreat can try to explain. Because, sadly, they are actually on the wrong team.

Plants vs humans. This war is just not a surviving one. But a praising one.

People cannot properly protect something if they don’t respect it. If they don’t know how valuable it is, how can they even care? How can they give their lives to something they don’t truly know?

But then not only people not care about the plant medicine. But they have got a total wrong focus of everything.

They have aimed their unconditional love and respect to the ones that are the real threat to everything. Us.

Humans are danger. We destroy nature for no reason at all. We bring disorder. That’s just how things are.

Yes, we can change and make things better. But for that we need to wake up. And good intentions are not enough.

Already so many years of activism and altruism and so on, and look where we end up? Just getting worse and worse.

So it is time to finally have the courage and say the things how they truly are.

Power to the plants. And then, only then, power to the people.
