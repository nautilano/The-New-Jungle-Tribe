We tend to normalize things as we keep living on a dream.

That lightning in the cloud for example. That's a fight happening right there. That's love, creation, intensity. That's someone there fighting the gods with no fear.

It's an answer. That shooting star and clouds that forms at your request, it's all a blessing. Even more than that. It's a a battle shout.

Everytime you follow your heart and make your ego run, that's when a little accident happens. Celebration of life. But once in the truth path, your soul never falls apart.

You are free and autonomous. You know when to party. You know when to stop. You know where to lay your head to rest.

You look for a sign? Look no more. Take what you can get from where you are now. That's the mission. The lesson. The beast for you to tame.

The beauty of the unpredictable. Ego doesn't like it. It doesn't want accidents. For you to get to the source, you need to let them happen.
