“Sorry, I got lost. But I came back. I know you understand though.

You see, a brought a friend. She’s looking for her dad. I know from what she told me, that he’s with you and your other folks.

I know that you don’t want me to keep looking for you. But darling, what would be of me if I don’t see you again?

You chose your life. And I have chose mine.

So I will use everything I have in hand to ignite the spark. For a moment, for a message. For one more moment together.

 You may have left me. But hope has not.”
