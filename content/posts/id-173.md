You know why "the underdog" is a popular main character in many movies? That is because they actually are that in real life.

The one who has to be followed doesn't want to be followed. This is one of the sad truths people got wrong since the beginning. There's no reason to follow anyone anyways, we all just need to realize we are all the same and all we have to do is wake up and notice, we already have everything to enjoy life.

The ones who know this, are they breaking their minds and bodies to explain and convince people to agree with them? Not at all. They are actually enjoying life. Sadly, nowadays there's only people listening to the ones who are screaming all  the time, instead of opening their hearts and listen to the tender whisper of life. Which is happening all the time.

At any second, at every moment there's the parade of life happening. There's really no need for any other extravagant show. Look at them, the birds, the clouds, the kids. Nothing to be done, nothing to be said.

Only protect and appreciate.
