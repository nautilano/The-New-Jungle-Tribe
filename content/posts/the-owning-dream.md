There is only one way our souls can be finally free. When we are fully aware of our power and we stop living the owning dream.

Yes, when we realize there's nothing to own. Nothing to hold. Nothing is there to lose.

We don't own nature. We don't own women. We just protect them. Same with knowledge. We are the ones in charge of keeping everything flowing through the right path for the future generations to come. Love doesn't end, it only expands.

We protect the river, we plant more trees. That's how it's commanded, that way the party keeps. More joy, more passion, more musicians to come. No interruption and no stealing of the show. We are just there appreciating it all, the love, the fire we keep. That's all the prize we need.
