If you happen to live in a city surrounded by wildlife then you would have a better view of the world we are living in. This is not our planet.

Of course. This is not a way for us to feel guilty and not worthy of living here, but instead, create collective consciousness to start making things better. But for that, we need to recognize our role and position in this matter.

Think about those lonely birds coming back at 8 pm when most of the flocks have already came back by sunset. You see? That’s a sign things have changed not only for them. But for everyone.

The natural order has already shifted in so many ways. So don’t feel alone and face reality with braveness. We are all together in this. Either we destroy everything or we find balance again.

Nothing will bring back the natural order because it was not supposed to keep like that since the beginning. Since the moment all different beings came here. We are supposed to transform, evolve and cooperate. Because no matter how many attempts there are to find somewhere else, there’s only one planet earth. So either we live with it and save it. Or we just die with it.
