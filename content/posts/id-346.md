"Because when I was there losing myself into the infinite vortex of my mind. She appeared and grabed my hand.

She stroked my head and said she has treated worst wounds than mine.

She was there, as the answer no one gets. But I got it, and now I have to pay.

But it's a work I do proudly. As I've been destroyed and can only save myself helping others.

Only seeing her in others. The light and happiness, and the love flowing. Only that can heal me too.

So yes, a truth nobody sees. The impossibility of chances. For them to believe, they need to meet themselves first."
