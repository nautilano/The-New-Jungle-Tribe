One good example of shamans losing control of their own play is them reflecting their fears on the people. This is what I call “fearflection”.

Particularly, during Ayahuasca, they would portray their own struggle as visions to their clients.

The funny thing is that this happens without them doing anything. Naturally, this is how Ayahuasca works. It’s showing people what’s going on in the environment. In the world, as in the mind. Sadly, people got the wrong idea by believing those visions are about them. When in reality, is a mix of everything.

We are all together in here, remember? Ayahuasca is meant to be taken in a sacred space with sacred people. These are the conditions for the show to be appreciated fully and without interruption.
