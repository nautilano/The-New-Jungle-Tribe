"I cannot lose any more time now. I need to hurry.

Yes it took me some time to realize, but now the path is clear and I don't have any doubts.

The intention and the cause has all filled my soul, now more than a human, I'm a bullet with a goal that can't be stopped.

Yes, maybe I will stagger, maybe I will fall, but the doors I opened now can no longer be closed. I see them, from far away, even with the eyes closed. Even with my mind clouded with distractions, light will come and clear the mist, warming my heart and soul"
