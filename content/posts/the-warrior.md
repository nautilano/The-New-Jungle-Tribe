Hundreds of lives have passed, hundreds of wars. A warrior lives in each of us, so better not to ignore it anymore.

At times when it’s all dark and the heart hasn’t healed from many wounds, he’s the one preventing demons from knocking our doors.

A voice of intuition. A voice of support. Be careful though, as many words you’ll hear won’t be of your taste at all.

Still he’s the one protecting us with so much passion and strength. A true lover of life. The one in charge of keeping the flame and showing us around.
