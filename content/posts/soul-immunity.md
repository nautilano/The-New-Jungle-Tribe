You know how hard was this to happen? How many lives went through multiple decisions and failures? How many accidents, coincidences, and dreams were followed to make the circumstances the way they are now?

Nothing is in vane and nothing is left to randomness. You think Gaia would leave her creation unsupervised? Of course not! She's always behind the curtain and always working to protect the party of life. She is always there. Always, at all times. Everything is perfectly designed for you to see.

You have 5 senses for a reason, and more if you wake up. But of course, if there's something Gods can't have, it's our souls. So, never ever make yourself believe that love is owned by someone. Love is for everyone that are willing to feel it.

Don't let yourself fall into the hands of black magic. The fear, blockages and dependence are all part of the ego strategy. Magic happens with freedom and sobereignty. It accepts, it doesn't judge. But it doesn' hold. It's on you to join the train of love. The party of life.

It's just there. Waving. Standing out of the crowd. Stop looking around. Some things shine and they are not gold, there are way better than that."
