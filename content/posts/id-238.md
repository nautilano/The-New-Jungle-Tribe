"You were a high promising soldier who got lost in the way to pursuit a lost cause.

Still, with all deviations and problems to face, you are still of good use to me.

This time you'll find the right moment to remember who you were. You'll see in him the same old good kid, with so much potential to release.

You'll notice he's dreaming, and how much he would enjoy the awakening. You'll see and maybe laugh from him. Think he's too innocent. But with a light you haven't seen in a while.

Then you'll realize what all of this is about. You got one more chance to act well. To redeem, and believe again, in the only true show we are all in.

You'll pass the baton proudly. You are as important as this warrior. Without you, without your failure, nobody would feel it enough and do it. Someone had to lose to make everyone win. At the end we are all in the same team.

That's the common goal, that's the force of love. We all have a moment to enjoy, we all have a moment to give back.

This time is for you to give back. The shortcut and the hack.

You know exactly what."
