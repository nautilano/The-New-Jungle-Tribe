“So many lives, so many stories, there was never a coincidence.

Since the moment I thought out of the box. I see now my path was already marked.

Call them Gods, call them fate, I just know who’s behind and that for me is enough.

The mother of all. The feminine energy. Without her it’s all dust and gas. But she’s the one who brought life.

So I work for light, but I’m also darkness.

Because for all of you to wake up first you’ll see me as bad.

Going against the current. Standing up when everybody is down. And saying the truth when lies sound cool. Yes. That’s me.

Darkness. I’m darkness for you. And maybe you won’t see it but i’m good. That’s alright. That’s just me doing my task, the one I’m actually starting to like.”
