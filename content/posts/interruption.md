“There has been an interruption to the party of life. There was love being made by the rhythm of the jungle sounds and got interrupted abruptly. Who dares to do that?

Of course the same ones that have been destroying my homeland. The ones that ironically think they are special, different or less evil. They don't realize they all come from the same place.

This time it was not for survival, not by accident. Not even by the vile human childish innocence. No. This time is plain stupidity driven by the ego.

To think they can take prominence in their made-up ceremonies? Ok I would expect that. To play on top of the music of life playing randomly sounds? Well. I would expect that too. But to start interrupting with the natural course of life? I would not tolerate that.

Not only my beloved plants are being exploited, wrongly used or judged. But they also start kidnapping my other children for no reason at all. They are supposed to be respected, protected and praised. Never take their lives without the natural calling of sacred sacrifice, where we all understand and follow the natural order of life.

Measures will be taken, and war will be declared. They will see now what playing with magic is about.”
