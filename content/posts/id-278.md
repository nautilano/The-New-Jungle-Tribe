There is a moment in Ayahuasca where you feel submerged into the ocean of wholeness. You are not drown, but feel like it. More like you can breath in the waters of knowledge. A magical feeling covers your whole body, slowly, as if searching for points to be healed. Points where energy got stuck, and light no longer comes in.

That's the call for you to to claim sovereignty of your body. You are in this realm to enjoy and fully celebrate your senses. You are here, so lucky to be here.

You are not just aware but feel the full capacity of your actions. You feel now the power, the will and fire you thought you didn't have.

It was there all the time, waiting for a spark, for a trigger that unchains the power of the source, the one you always had on the palm of your hands.
