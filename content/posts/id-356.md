So yes, stand proud and tall when struggle comes. And keep in mind it's all for the best. It's all for you to know where you stand for, and which is the side you'll never fall from.

Cause emotions based on truth are never wrong. It's us processing the whole spectrum of love. Trying to tune in. Trying to find balance. Trying to wake up from all dreams.

As long as we keep trying, we'll always get a hand of support. You have the clouds, the stars, all together to give you signs. You just need to notice them. Open your heart and let them work on your youl."
