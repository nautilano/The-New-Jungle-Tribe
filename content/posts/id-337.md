The past you helped the present you. It knew before being fully conscious

It took the risk, more than anyone would.

Don’t turn your back to him now.

It knew exactly what to do to trigger you. To ignite the small spark needed to fully realize your full potential. To know the real you. The only mirror showing the true reflection.

You see? There was always one true friend helping you all the time. Nothing more than yourself.

So you were never alone. Never left to drift. You were always on the side of wisdom. Of experience. You needed to fail to realize. You needed to lose to appreciate. It was all for your own good.

You were always walking with an angel. The only one wanting the best for you. True intentions. Never selling your soul to anyone.

It’s only if you fight. If you no longer rcognize yourself. That’s when you fall. And that’s when you come back too. You always come back. You have to know that’s a law.
