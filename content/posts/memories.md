"Theres a door I like to enter. The door of memories.

In times in battle, when body hurts but the soul is intact. I see the stars and close my eyes. The silence. The peace. The comfort.

A image draws in the darkness and suddenly colors everything up. Your face shows up and my heart warms up. The laughs, your touch. The comfiest hands and heavenly lap.

How I wish I would come back. Yet, it's the fuel I'm using to survive. For one more time, one more chance. One more truce in this realm, to find a piece of light in the middle of this madness. To hold it more, slow time and take my lover out.

This is not simply motivation. This is me being driven. From the source, all the power and magic, filling my lungs and putting my soul on fire."
