There's only one truly state of realization of men.

And it's not with money, nor women, nor power. A man is fully realized once it achieves the sacred universal achievement. His contribution to the cause. His answer to the call and protection of the source.

What comes with this state is pure soul immunity and inner peace of mind. He no longer lives in a dream, but shaped reality a bit more to the conditions he likes.

A man that lives in the truth. His eyes can see trough the curtains of the show and can identify masks made of fear. He has mastered tolerance since his heart is shaped in patience.

A man can be poor and live a simple life in a village, and the birds will not stop singing. He finds joy in the calmness and peace of his world, the one we live all and he contributed to save, and will be gratefully reminded of that until the end of his days.
