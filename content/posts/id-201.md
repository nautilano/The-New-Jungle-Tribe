Every great warrior has a magic Merlin who has found his way through the darkest mist, and has got used to face the worst demons of them all. Their role is so important as the warriors themselves. The Merlins are probably the most dedicated students of the plant teachers and their relationship is so mystical and powerful that even Gaia can't comprehend.

Lucky for her, they are in the good side, and they will always work as allies in the big battle of love. Even without having the divine protection that usually warriors are granted, they will still live and die in her honor.

These Merlins are not the people you can just simply find. They find you when you need them the most. And they don't look for money, fame or status. They just like working with you. They are lovers of their craft, of their magical science.

There's only one way to recognize them for real. They will also need your help. But you won't be able to help them. They know and they will be perfectly fine.

They know their duty is to assist you, so you are able to fully perform yours.
