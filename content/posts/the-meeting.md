"I've been waiting a long time for this moment. Finally, the moon and fortune grant me this ever longing pleasure."

"I told you when you stop looking, I would appear again"

"Well, I hope, with all due respect, you tell me what happened with the warrior. The kid you told me to choose to hold the medicine"

"He got killed"

"Ok, did he get to share and spread awareness of the medicine? Did he grow up and got to enjoy a life with his family?"

"No, he was killed the day after you gave it to him"

"How can you allow this? Is this the kind of game you like playing with?"

"You understand I'm also part of this. Even though it seems I do mistakes, they are no mistakes. What we saw in this kid, no one can takes, no one can predict. Life comes and goes, but the soul remains intact. Yes, he is the warrior for a reason. He needs to see see all the painting, go through all the library, to know by himself and let no one trick him. Not even myself."

"I really don't understand. But I hope you are done with these games, otherwise you lose and we all lose too"

"Don't worry my love. You already did your task, and you are having the life you asked. Safe, protected, the last barrier of them all. Let others defend you too. You already triggered the first spark. Feel proud about that.

Now go back to your magic swamp"
