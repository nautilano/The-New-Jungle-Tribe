“I am not a shaman. But I am a man.

I am a combination of principles and values. A mix of experience and openness.

I respect traditions. But most importantly, I understand people.

So I never close my mind to new projections of the mysterious life. I am a fan. So this is what keeps me alive.

I know the medicine does not have limit. This is what I want you to realize.

I'm not important. I'm just a man. I know my duties. I do my work.

You see? The medicine is big, and I'm small. I can only get that high, enough to help you not to fall.

That's all I do. This is me already working. This is it. Simpler than it seems."
