“Is the jungle that heals. Not me.

I am just an assistant. A worker. I protect. I just preserve.

I make sure it’s all well set and safe. That there’s nothing to worry. Nothing will interrupt.

I make sure the whole scenario is ideal for the most magical setting in the universe.

The most beautiful show in life. One I am just  lucky to witness.

That’s why I am serious on this.

Because I know the importance. I know how much is on play.

This is you. Not just any soul. Is a warrior finding its way. The independence of life happening in front of my eyes.

What a wonderful piece of art. Nothing was less earned than this. This is all well deserved. Is a work of years, of many lives. A conjunction of accidents, of bravery and battles.

I am seeing the strongest force of the universe canalizing into this moment. Enough for a simply human to understand.

I appreciate and I am ready to give it all. Because I believe and trust.

I’m just lucky. That’s how I remain humble. And that’s how I keep. Being grateful all the time.”
