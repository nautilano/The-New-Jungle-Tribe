You shall be careful of the amount of tricksters you will meet along your way.

But be careful though. As the most dangerous you will meet is yourself.

Remember we are all victims. Already there’s a constant fight in our souls. So don’t be so naïve and always keep a feet in each side of the road.

Yes, I know you feel it. I know your heart tells you one thing. But not accepting the harsh true  is already all the sign you need.

Be open, bold and fearless to see all flavours of life. The good and the bad. As you keep walking your path you’ll learn to analyze and decide faster. Intuition most of the time tries ignoring one side in order to follow what’s more familiar to you. This doesn’t mean is the right choice. Nevertheless, it’s all ok. Do it, and if you fail you will learn. But careful you shall be, as in the game there is only unlimited options as you keep leveling up. If you keep failing you will just keep falling into a never ending trap and will be  harder to leave as you keep going by.

So go for the new. At least you know that if you fail you are learning something else.
