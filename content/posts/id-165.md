The one thing the jerk or ego don't expect us to do is that we all realize we are living a dream. It's the final face off in which the mirror breaks in pieces, and there's only plain reality at front.

That's it. After that the train departs and there's no way to come back. No more delays, no more deviations. It's on you now, you have the choice. Whether you let your conscience rule or keep being a slave of your own dreams.

Only that, only accepting and recognizing our human foolish nature is when we can shake our sedentary soul and start tuning in. Only Gaia knows all, and she was wrong at first.

Nobody is really safe. Nobody is separated from the big picture. Either we all wake up, or we stay in the dream.
