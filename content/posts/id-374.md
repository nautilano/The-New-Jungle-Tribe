Nothing will be the same. And you know, I'm ok with that.

I already saw destruction in and out. I already saw the light fighting the darkest of the conditions. What can be worse? Nothing. I just want a place where no harm can be made. I just want to protect. I don't wanna see more stormy minds and empty eyes. At least not without the power to help.

So then, in that case, things may change.

Only if blessing follows, if the sky understands. It's only when people trust and open up, that's when you enjoy the party of life.

So yes, I believe, as I've been given green light. By the one and only force that is working day and night. The one holding everything together while creating wonderful pieces of art. The one inspiring us, the one singing a song for us. The one giving us answers all the time.

That's my boss. And I would die for her. Yes, I may fail, and still she'd be intact. Still she would be shinning and living, and  she'll be giving rewards to the ones who trust. Because in the path of light, the path of the source, is love and only love that goes and comes"
