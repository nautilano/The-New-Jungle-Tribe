"Even without me, I want you to enjoy life, keeper of the flame.

Yes, it's sad I'm leaving, but you'll be fine and live a happy life. I'm a timebomb and where I go you can't go. With so much risk and so many demons, my focus would be you and not my purpose.

Do what you know she may do. Live as it's supposed to. Free and wild, like a bird, like the one you always were.

Never held, never tamed, always free and independent. That's you and that's how you keep the flame. No one will take it from you, cause you have the trigger in your soul. Your mind is the key, your body the machine. It's all in you. It's all you.

I pray for the day you realize, you are her. She counts on you, she depends on you. You start and keep the fire, you are in charge, you always were."
