He rides like there's no world to come back. Only one purpose, only one goal pumping his heart.

To see you. To come, knock and pray for one more chance. One more time to see the stars, one more time to feel alive.

You may not know, but this man loves you like the Sun loves fire. Free and conscious, with his soul agonizing, he feels time and death surrounding, yet he's ready to give them one more battle.

Yes, he can lose, he can win, but he knows he will love until the end. And he will come back loving again"
