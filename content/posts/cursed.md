"I see now when I look back, I have been cursed.

Because I see your faces, each one of you, and can't help but feel each one of your fates.

No, I didn't choose this. But I guess this pain will follow me until I get freed.

This feeling though, as bad as it seems, is actually needed, for us to stop this sin.

All together, no more unconscious. No more sleeping. We wake up our feelings, we wake up our senses.

Now there's no one taking control but us, no one covering the sun. We tune in and feel the light coming. We can feel there's no stopping.

We are lucky the source is open, we bow to always keep in humbleness. "
