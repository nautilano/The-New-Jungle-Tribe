Why do we sing? To honor the silence. To tune in collectively. We sing when we already heard a lot. And since we know how beautiful everything is, it's time for us to pay some tribute. By singing and playing, we show what we learned. We show we are worthy of being part of the big orchestra but still remaining humble, for when the time comes to be silent, and give space to the true master.

Like animals, who are always tuned to the party of life. They don't have many strange thoughts or ideas clouding their minds. They are just comfident singing and playing their parts. Every sound and vibration is a ritual of life. They don't overthink. They don't feel afraid. They are all the time aware.

Living has become their field of expertise. They are the truest artists of the source. They don't need to be told what to do. They already know when they feel it, without dreams or complications, they just exist.

That's what music is for. For us to run away from the collective dream, and enjoy a moment of focus in a world full of distractions.
