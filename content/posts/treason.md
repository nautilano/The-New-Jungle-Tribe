"How can it be that men believe more in themselves than in mother nature?

Don't you see every day with your eyes? Who gives the light? Who gives the day and night?

Where is your respect? You have gone totally reckless and went against everything I commanded.

Not only you destroy my home, where you have been invited as guests, but also start exploting the magic vine under my name.

You are profiting with souls using lies and imposing fear. This is not what the medicine is about.

No time in history I saw so much distortion of reality. And to make it worse, you are using it as a tool to open doors that are not suitable for you to handle.

Who gave you the power? Who gave you the authorization?

You think this is a kids game and that anyone can play on the field?

Ok you can do it, and burn yourself with it, but to bring other souls to the game is not something I will tolerate.

You are kidnapping minds, lives and dreams. The punishment for this goes beyond you can imagine.

I don't care about you, but I care about the others."
