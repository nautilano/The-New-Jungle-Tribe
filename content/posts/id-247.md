"I entered to a world full of darkness. Huge skyscrapers touching not the sky, but a big mirror. Everywhere you look there was a reflection. Nothing to see but myself.

I kept walking as I was trying to understand. There was none, no sign of others. Only me, alone and lost.

After this I was dragged onto a rollercoaster. An exhibition of different worlds. No music, and no sound, I was just eyes and melting, my skin on different matters.

I saw all chances, all possibilities, all in fractions of seconds. How actions are connected, how decisions influenced. And everytime I saw an outcome. A different reality unfolding. Never ending. As if love has made sure to survive, even on the dumbest hands.

![](images/aqojfoo5dvsw7j0xwrpofwva8dgqrwdfzhhhdazddbhhpecawmunxfmwfewczkzx4m9z8opnokcpewjfxwpt1zux.jpg)

Then silence ended with the most beautiful noise. The quietest peace. Only to make space for the most beautiful voice singing angelical notes.

That was the end. No more worlds to see. Or I wish that was the case.

One more world was left. And my eyes have not been the same since.

A world full of triangles. Too strange and hard to process.

![](images/aqmirxhawtbee3gcpkblsrbu1k3yeuqyx9pfladu1fxuqhdxja5j4dtqh0fmyr8uq_x-w6cr64mbnsvl54zc37du.jpg)

Triangles coexisting, living, all making more and more. I see then everything has a concept. Before we are humans. Before the world exists. First there's a triangle of concepts, then everything is shaped, and things just need to be adjusted.

This is not the adjustment we all run away from. But the one we should embrace. We can float, fluctuate and vibe around it but we never leave. Otherwise we find unbalance and fall from the road, and then we struggle all the way again to get back in track.

This is what the medicine is about. Love has no limit, and this is the whole concept of it. When we lose it we lose our minds. No other thing can be the same. This concept is the truth, the one connected to the source, the one we are all meant to protect and embrace. No hard feelings and no reasons to be afraid."
