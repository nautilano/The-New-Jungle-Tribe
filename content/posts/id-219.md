You think that's a coincidence? That's no coincidence at all. That's an invented word for the fools to keep dreaming. To think everything is random, how easy everything is.

But reality is way more simple, and way more complicated. But complicated in terms of believing these things. For you to be the kid you once were. Not the immature adult you are now.

Remember the hard part is just taking the plunge. Everything else will just come once you are in the sea, swimming and surviving on your own. Only to see at the hardest times that you were never alone.

After that you'll recognize all paths taken, all chances missed. All people you lost, all people you forgot. You'll see this is a long road we all go and cross at the same time. We are all runners of the same marathon life, and there's no one, but many to keep up.

So if you help one soul in one race, that move will give you points when you less expect. This is a race where we all lose and we all win. There's no one prize, no one winner.

You may fall and you may be tired. At some point carry others will not be rewarding. And that's where you'll need to use the only power up allowed. Trust.

You never lose if you always trust. No matter how many times you are proven otherwise.
