"I won't let you go. Even if you took a different road.

I'll go and look for you, even with prohibitions. I'll break all rules with no conditions. All to see you one more time. To make sure you are fine and see me who I am.

I'm not the one who forgot about you, not the fool who couln't see what's true.

Who I was then if not myself? Just another fool with folded eyes. Yet you were there and saw the truth. You the fortune itself, you were only light.

Still I was so dumb and couldn't see the luck. The big surprise of life. You were only love and now I can't but feel the misery I am.

I'm all giving now. I don't ask. But I want you alive and happy, enjoying how it should be. And I wanna see it. Before I'm done. Before I go and don't come back."
