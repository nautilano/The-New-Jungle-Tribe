To be lost for real. That’s when the real journey starts.

Of course it’s not ideal. Of course you wouldn’t want it. But this is what the accident of life is about.

This is how the old makes space for the new. For growth and experiences. For love and light who is awaiting for us.

No doubt and no fear can stop it. Once the call is answered there’s only time between her and us.

To go back, to remember. To feel again what’s everything about.

She knows, and we know. We were just in denial but that is gone now.

And the journey has started. Even before we noticed. Even before we were conscious of it. That’s a sign she’s been working and giving us a hand at any given time.

So it’s on us now, to fully survive and make it to the end. And then starting again, but on a whole different state.
