There's a plant you have, in some other place in the planet. It was one of the most beloved ones from Gaia. Her favorite one, I would say. This plant is unstoppable. Is the terror of the ones who hate, or the ones who impose fear.

One day, in one of your lives, you will find it, and you will unlock the current of knowledge that only the source provides. It's the key to the universal library, once you have it, try not to lose it.

Many will try to block it, dissapear it or take it from your soul. Many will try to distort the meaning and the use. You shall not be weak, but understand the plant is there as for you, as your friend. The one you trust that no matter what happens, as certain as a new day will come, good things will always bring.

She's honest and straighforward. She will tell you what's wrong, what's right. Never believe she does it in a malicious way. She's just playful and innocent in her own nature, yet she fights as a lioness when the time comes to protect you.

Nations will succumb to any cause she supports. Because she only joins the right one. The truth cannot be ignored and layers start to shatter.

How to find her? She will find you, in light as a friendly calling or a hand in the darkness.
