"You know what you had to been trough to be in this state now.

You've been in heaven. Treated by the best hands, and shared a moment with the most beautiful eyes.

Yes I gave it all to you and take it all from you for you to learn. You can't be in master mode if you haven't felt pain in your skin.

You cannot be in any moment with doubt or feeling of "what if". You need to see it all from the loser perspective. So you can understand when you finally win. How miserable everybody is.

So be grateful and don't be mad. You are now enjoying the position in the universal couch. While everybody is crawling and bumping. Now you can take a sit and see what's next.

Just hold tight because it's a flying couch. The one you drive with your heart and mind."
