"You are continuation. You are the sign things will go on after I'm gone. You are the answer to the mysteries of life. You are the sign that Gaia's love is infinite. Never stopped, never retracted, always expanding.

Soldiers will born and die, but souls will live. That's our job, that's the goal of our sacrifice. So be proud and look ahead. Cause we are the proof the image is bigger and the show never ends, no matter what happens everyday.

You need to trust and know good will happen. Confidence turns on the antenna of success. So if you are stranded and crambling, turn on the antenna in your head and you will be on the way. No matter how dark, how rainy or how many enemies you face. At any moment, if you look ahead and see inside, you will feel it. That natural and outwordly call in which your soul is being driven."
