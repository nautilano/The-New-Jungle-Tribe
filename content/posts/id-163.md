Everyone and everything has changed because there's injustice happening. The mother of all injustices. We being lied.

In this state we don't even know what's right, what's wrong. And our minds ruled by ego and filled with confusion creates a never ending fight that infects and spreads everywhere around.

This is the fight we are all in. The war we didn't choose yet we have to take a side. The mind, or the feelings. I would choose the feelings. Because if ideas and concepts come in hundreds, feelings come in handy. It's not so complicated, it's not so hard. Yet ego is a strong enemy and always wants to survive and be in charge.

Still the feelings of truth never stop shaking it's cage. Even minds in darkness cannot live with some degree of light. That's all we need, from there we can make juice. Cause light and love spreads, never ends. Once you open the right tunnel, and trigger the right bullet, the good will come at its fullest.

You may side eye, try to silence or even ignore, but not for too long. Time will happen and even your life may go, but the truth will always call your soul.
