"Yes I knew you would have these doubts at some point.

Look, If I was wrong once, anyone can be. Remember they are all my children, they are all me, and their ego and the dreams they have will make them think they come from their own world, and they cannot be understood.

Wrong. That's why I'm sending you to this battle. Only you who has loved us since the beginning and suffered infinite times due to our own nature. Only you who have bleed as multiple times as we did.

So don't hesitate anymore. If one loved you and hate you now, anyone can. If she's the one you think is worth fighting demons and saving the world from darkness then go for it. She's the trigger and the tiger. She's the final version of myself in this world. She's the last stand of my nature, the accident of love, and if she gets lost everything will be gone.

This is the fight you have been preparing for. All along, all those lives, all those unsuccessful stories of love, they were all for you to know how to end this.

You want some rest? You want to finally enjoy? This is it. But I ask you for one more battle. Fight and hold as you know. I will be with you now and I will be with you after.

She just needs to see and she will notice. But for that you need to unveil the curtains, destroy the stage and end the dream she's on.

Yes, she may fight for it, as she’s a warrior too. But remember you have something they don’t. Me on your team. "
