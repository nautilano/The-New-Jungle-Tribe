Doors open at the same time for all. You need to be wise to recognize and act fast before they close.

This doesn’t mean being impatient. Not at all. This is you always being aware and calm.

Already you have been asleep and lost. Already saw the opportunities go by. That’s ok. That’s alright.

For us to finally beat fear and set free we’ll require so much time in the work. A long process of wins and falls. But everytime, with every chance hit and missed, you’ll be more aware of the experience.

Yes, it may hurt more. Every attempt will feel heavier to carry, and every outcome more difficult to handle. But keep in mind that in the long run you are just advancing.
