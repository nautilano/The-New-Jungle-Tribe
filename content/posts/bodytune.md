Touch yourself. Feel your body. Feel what you are made of. Feel with intention, with recognition, with magic familiarity and no restriction.

Each part of your body is perfectly designed to receive love. Every cell, every inch of your skin has a receptor, a magical antenna directly connected to the source.

She's there, all the time transmitting, all the time singing the beautiful chords of life. Yet sometimes we forget and get still, focusing on vague things.

She's playing, so we dance. Move and feel what your soul is made of. The purest expression of love flowing in the beautiful space. That space, that tiny piece of reality, is owned by you. You matter, you rule and you influence. All your actions, all your thoughts play a good role in this big number.

So don't get distracted and get the party on. Adjust your radio-mind and tune to the right station. You see? Can you feel? It's everywhere. Shaking every cell, warming it up, lighting the fire. This is how you are on track. You jump, you run. You fly.

This is how she feels it, now you can feel it too.
