Nobody is safe from hate. Nobody is safe from the dark. From the moment we are born into this world, into this reality, we are all exposed to the same ups and downs of the game.

All the tricksters, all the true guides. They are all there. Some of us, unfortunately, don’t know how to play.

And so ego comes. The worst villain of them all. Together with fear it turns the player into an unresponsive npc.

If we fail into that trap we cannot play the game anymore. Worst than losing, we become encapsulated and lost into the darkness of the code. Ego, and only hate can come.

So once you are free and break the chains you finally understand what the game is about, and how we are capable of everything.

There’s no limit in our imagination. And reality just shapes according to it.

You see love is the answer to all. And how wrong we were to ever think it was not.
