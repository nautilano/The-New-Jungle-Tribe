Simple as that. There’s no reason to complicate anymore. We’ve been fooled into believing there’s a side of every story. Of course there is. But that doesn’t mean we should distort the values and the truth. That is basically the cause of every modern problem we have in the world.

Before we were ignorant savages. Now we are smart fools. There’s no reason to keep being puppets anymore. We have everything to start changing things for the good but it’s only us who is stopping it.

The damn ego. It’s so hard for us to accept the harsh truth. Just one big slap it’s what it takes, and then pain will go away. Just one is enough to be awake, and to listen and to feel. And you will see the picture was bigger that you could ever see in your dreams.

Reality is way more beautiful than any made up  story. We just have to act, and surrender and act. That is how we learn, and that’s how we advance. Losing and winning. In and out.

But first surrender and accept. There’s only good and bad.
