The current Ayahuasca industry doesn't prive itself from moral incongruities, done by the same people that run it, and the same that consume it.

Some think it's a feared drug, some think it's indigenous depravation. Some think is not good to charge so much money for it, that should be for everyone and so on.

Truth is nobody has a clue of what they are saying. Acknowledge first, that the world is stupid, and from this we can move on.

But Ayahuasca is so powerful that would exist even in a world of oblivion. Where holders of the truth are falling behind more and more.

How to keep then? How to approach to the magical truth of the medicine without being fooled by representatives of the jerk?

Well. The answer is pretty simple. You just need to observe. In a world full of subtitles, people forgot to see the eyes. The actions, the ideals. They are all ignored now. As if you can buy healing, magic and love in a platform. Based on pictures and reviews as if we are all on a competition.

Ayahuasca is way bigger than this. But this is how the world has got to know it. There must be a reason. And that reason is love.

Yet the ones who need it more are the ones getting rejected. Usually people with severe mental problems such as schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, get deprived from the opportunity of meeting with Gaia. But who commanded this? Not Gaia, but the people running a business.

Yes, if you want, you can be a client and get what you are paying for. Hints and small pieces of what they can produce. You can get a chain of gold, but you'll never understand where the gold comes from.

The Ayahuasca world should then make a clear difference between clients and warriors. You can be a client anytime, any moment, there are plenty of options to choose. But for a warrior you need way more than that.

Only true warriors can get the real truth. Which is preparation and self awareness. You don't need psychedelics for ego death. You just need to accept your dumbness. After that you are ready for everything. The doors of knowledge and doors of foolness open for you. You may fail but at least you are conscious. You are now clearing the path instead of tripping on the stone over and over.

Healing is a complete work over our body, mind and spirit. With awareness of our feelings and senses. With integrity, principles and morals. This is only complicated if you try to run from it. You already know what's good for living, you just need to start living.

And with all the crazyness of the world, hate and ego, it's understandable people fall into the identity abism where they don't know who they are and what are they doing. And this is the case with almost everyone in the world.

True information will always be there, and Ayahuasca will be available to people in many ways. Even with constant blackouts and storms of darkness. She will be there waiting for you, with no more lies and demons around.
