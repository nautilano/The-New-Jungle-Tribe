"Fear is the biggest tool of the jerk. His name is not even worth mentioning.

He will use that tool until the end of it's days. He's addicted to it. Expert and master of deceiving. He knows all the power in it.

Fear not only changes people, but turns them against each other. Never finding a path, a way out, just running in circles, crashing themselves without a chance to stop.

Even seeing, even listening. Their minds will be so clouded only responding to the storms of ego. Never in control anymore, just stranded souls in a factory of multiple dreams.

The ones influenced by the jerk, will live on a multiple layered illusion where it's going to be almost impossible to clear out. There will not allow other minds to enter to their well protected garden. They will use the skills and manners necessary to keep enchanting others and believe in the same dream they are.

Fear is  going to be your biggest enemy. The real virus of the world. Sometimes very close to you, sometimes very far, but always there, trying to invade your soul. So be ready to face it and show who is the boss"
