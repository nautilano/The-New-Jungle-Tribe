"No matter the outcome. You have to know at the end who's losing here.

Not the jerk, who at the end will end up the same way he started. With nothing. Neither you all, my beloved sleepers, who will just wake up from a dream to see the new day. You will see the sunrise, enjoy the sunsets, the stars and moonlight like never before.

Not Gaia, who is just working on what she loves, being herself.

Remember, for some to win, others need to lose. And that's me, in this life.

Yes, I'm the one sacrificing a comfy dream for a life of service. Now, don't feel sad for me. And don't take me for special either.  We should, as always, differentiate and separate things. Tho I'm happy for working for the biggest cause of all, I can't help but to think how much of a loser I am.

For me to get this knowledge I had to fail in the most miserable ways. And some of those losses are big ones, the ones you can't recover, the ones you can't revert.

Why I say this then? For you to understand you are obligated to enjoy life at its fullest. For all the warriors who sacrifice their lives, and for yourself. Live with the time others couldnt. Enjoy the chances and opportunities others can't. Nobody can take this from you. If a path opens to you is for you to walk it. Others will be helped by themselves anyway. Trust in the work and do what you can.

Unless a call comes, but that's another story."
