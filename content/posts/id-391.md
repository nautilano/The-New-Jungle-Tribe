“I choose to see the good things while on the path of truth, because I know regardless of misfortunes, I may be hurt but never wrong.

I choose not to run away. Not from things I know they are needed. To fall in order to learn. How else is there to fully live? I face fear and this way I always win. My soul is ready to take the punch. To take the bullets. Every attempt to beat me and every attempt to push me are just ways for them to fail. As in misery, as in their dreams.

And even if the dark comes I know it won’t be long. Because many times it happened, and many times I saw the moon and the sun come. They never left. They were just enjoying time. As they always do. As we should do too.

So I see the path glowing and all the blessings coming. I choose to embrace and not to look away. I am brave and take it as it comes. As a beautiful accident. As a beautiful hug in the soul.”
