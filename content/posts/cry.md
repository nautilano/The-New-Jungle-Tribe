"It's ok you can cry, cry as much as you want.

I know I've been tough, but hey, you've been through all, and it was all for your own good.

I didn't mean it like this, you know? It was all supposed to be like this. These are the conditions for life.

Fire, suffer, intensity. We all go through this, but not all of us leave from this. It takes courage, wisdom and integration to be able to switch reality and start enjoying it.

Now you see how beautiful it is? That's because you know the very end of the opposite. You already know and you survived! You kept your soul and mind immune, invictus.

So, cry, cry, it's ok not to cheer up now. This is a moment to cry proudly, with eyes up, closed and open. Now you fear nothing. You see now what darkness is, is just you switching the lights of your room. And then you choose what you do, party or love, it's all up to you.

Come, my dear, I hug you. This is you, this is your body, you are alive, you are here. Now you feel what matter is, what everything is built of. Now you understand and you can join the party, but you never stop being awe.

You just like to observe, sit and listen. This is my show for you. You are not just part of it, you are my main attendee. What the art is about if not for the observer? Nothing, you are to me like I am to you.

Now enjoy, enjoy at your throne while I am performing the show.

That's why you bought the ticket for"
