There’s a world of concepts, a whole different world where values are key. We also have it here but we choose not to see it.

In Ayahuasca you cannot ignore it. You cannot separate it because Ayahuasca is everything. Its happening every second, in every situation.

So in a ceremony, why separate it? Why don’t you embrace it?. Ayahuasca is connection, so this is the best chance to be at peace with concepts. To be clear and honest. To look back proudly and move forward undoubtedly.

Only in peace, only with comfort, only with understanding. That’s how we all get healed.

The past is chaos. The past was dark. But now we have the chance to choose. The power to change and control outcomes. No longer we are afraid of being judged, or being rejected. Now we all see where we are standing, and where we are all going.
