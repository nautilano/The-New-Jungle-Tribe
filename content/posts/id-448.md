What do all natural medicine have in common? They require the 4 elements some in more than degree than others.

But there’s one in particular that requires special attention. Fire. Whether as a concept, as the spark igniting inside us, or the actual element in our realm.

Either case it has to be treated with care, as it is capable of destroying as well as creating.

It is the beginning and it is the end. So make sure your relationship with it comes with respect and appreciation.

It is a tool to use but can also be your curse. So balance and awareness. That is the way for fire to be tamed.
