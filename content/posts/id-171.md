How can you fully jump into the infinite void if you are afraid of not coming back? Fear is gonna be there next to you, slowing you down, adding weight to your back. It will occupy every inch of your self and kidnap the command and control of your mind. Yes, fear is natural at some point. But that's only at the beginning, before you take the jump into the unknown. Because that's how it's going to be. Unknown until it's known, by you. Anything I can say won't be any closer to what you will experience, but it will be the same feeling. The same train of love.

This is what I know, and this is why I'm writing this. Information is God. The mere action of sharing is connected to the source. So as long as it's true and comes from the heart, there's no reason to doubt, no reason to be afraid. Because it's all being done into the jurisdiction of love. And there's no space for unneeded demons or extra complications. Things are actually very simple. If they are not, that's on us, and that's why we need to heal and get back to work. The soldier work, the one we don't get tired of. The one we live, enjoy and die for.
