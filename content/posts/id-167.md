"He will appear at the right times with the same strengh he makes love with. That inner and passionate fire that only comes from the source. He'll appear, and you may not know, but later when you look back you'll realize. He was always there. He let you live, he let you learn. He knew the exact moments when to trigger a memory, a feeling or even a coincidence. There's no such thing as this. Everything we are is an expression of the purest love, that begins and continues. It doesn't end. Love is happening. We are already happening. That's why everything is already working on that way, the way of the creation.

He will appear in the black panther that you see in a cage, to see in his eyes the most intense fearless bluntness that only comes with the desire for freedom. He will make things happen and let you fall in the trapping hole of injustice. He'll make you feel alone and miserable because that's the way of life for someone who is awaking. Truth is uncomfortable, but that's the only way for you to leave the cage. Others fall in a dream and just keep believing their own reafirming lies, moving from a cage to a castle in the skies.

But for you there's no limit, there's no enclosure that can confine your soul and mind. I know it will be hard, but one day you'll see why. "
