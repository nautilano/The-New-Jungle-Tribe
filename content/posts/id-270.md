"A demon has come to me and realized we are not enemies at all. How can we be? If we are all victims. We all go through the same struggle of finding who we are. Some of us do, some others get lost in the way.

You can never hate anyone if you understand the circumstances. After this you can never judge because you know how hard the way is. Because you've already been through this, you experienced, and you left victorious. Hence you can't help but feel empathy for them.

But no pity and no blame. Each one is owner of it's own fate.

What we can do is stop demonizing things that are not, and so things will be clearer, and maybe easier. But ego can't stop itself. It will always find ways to crawl over our hearts and scracht our souls. That's when awareness and acceptance comes. Always facing the truth. Never running from it.

We can all be friends. Yes, we can, even if takes a punch in the face. For some to understand, for some to sleep and wake up again."
