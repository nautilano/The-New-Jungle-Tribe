“I feel the weight of the world on my shoulders. I feel the hate that is burning on people’s souls. I see now, that if I do nothing I will become one of them. Because hate is just the absence of love. Therefore we need to act and always fight under the name of love.

I won’t become one of them tho. No, I won’t. Because I know where I come from, and my memories of love are always strong. Therefore I know where to go and what to look for.

That magic feeling of love. We all get it. Some forget it. We should not stop, we should not give up. Cause love is just there on its waiting. To be claimed, to be spread .

It was always there, it never left. You were just seeing a different way.”
