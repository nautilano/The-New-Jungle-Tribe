"There is a girl you need to save. But you will need to realize how. Because honestly, I don't even know how.

This woman is me, just asleep in a dream. And you know, if the dream seems comfy, we don't want to wake up.

The worst thing is that she's been trapped into the worst hands of all. In the same hands I once fall.

So to free her, you will need to go against a whole system. A system that has been portraying itself as the good. As the revolution. But in reality they are just aimed to trap people even more. How smart right? Or how dumb I would say.

But this is the worst supplanting of them all. They are kidnapping souls and using the medicine for it. Yes I keep closing doors, but if I close them all, there won't be any more light going on, and then I will have to shut down the school. No more earth, no more love. No more continuation.

Therefore I come to ask for your assistance. Only you, who can go against all.

You, the natural rebel. I've followed your path through many lifetimes, and I know about your intentions, your values, what you live for.

You have now come back in this life to the source, to the place of truth, after wandering all earth. And you got to be in the same place, in the same time as this girl. Maria. Who is the one in charge of keeping the truth and more importantly, share it to the world. She is a moving light herself, glowing everywhere she walks to. She has this magical enchantment flowing through her self that will make everyone follow and believe her.

She's the one who will rise her voice and lead humanity to a common understanding, where we can all gratefully and peacefully get to the train of love.

But you are the one who will set her free. This is the last mission I have for you. Everything you ask will be granted as long as you finish this last task.

I'll give you all tools and make my move in the moments I can. But remember, these won't be the same battles you are used to, but you will still need your warrior skills. You are not only facing humans, but demons and all representations of fear. You are facing the jerk himself.

So for this I will need you to be the real you, the inner kid, the one kid that knows what everybody needs, specially Maria. And that is love and only love."
