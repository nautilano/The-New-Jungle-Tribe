Of all the allies we can have in this world. There’s one that stands out exponentially. Those are cats.

Cats, same as dogs, come from a different realm where they are rulers. And the felines, particularly, are the ones actually ruling this one too.

They come to us because they have a reason to. And they would actually leave without hesitation, without even looking back.

The myth about having 7 or 9 lives is actually misinterpreted, as so many things in life. You see, most of the cats look exactly the same for a reason. So a cat of the same color, can be actually the cat you met before. If that cat has already passed away. Be sure you are seeing that cat again in this new cat. Their eyes are portal to the same universe. It’s on you to believe in it.
