Even among the distractions. Among the darkness and chaos. When you feel alone and lost in hell with the worst demons you can imagine.

She’s there. She’s always there. Sometimes in the eyes you don’t expect. Sometimes in the only spark of light remaining inside your being. You have to believe. She’s also finding her way.

Even when you think you are divided. No, darling. You have her in you. The same power inside every plant and every bird and so on. We all have the same power of love.

So what you have to do is to endure and keep on. At some point, at the right time, the right door will open and you will know what to do.

And don’t worry at all. She finds her ways trough her teachers too. So there is gonna be a long line of teachings that you already got along the way. Sometimes without noticing, sometimes very aware of it.

So relax and get action. Find the right balance and trust deep in your heart that things will go the right way.

Because there’s only one game, and only one real boss. And she knows to appear when you need the most.
