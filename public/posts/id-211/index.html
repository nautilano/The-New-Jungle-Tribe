The jaguar

"I've embraced the power of the Jaguar. The look, the focus, the temple. The calm and pace that only comes with experience. No other way I can face all these demons and the minds they have entrapped with them. Only with power and capacity of defense, I can go and get back to recharge. There's no retreat, only constant pursuit. From different sides, different directions. They won't know from where I come. They will try and look on their books, their manuals, but all they find is reflections of their own traumas. Their teachers, tired and old, will only go to sleep and say .  Something they knew, but their dream always kept them comfy, ignorant and valiant. Better for me. This make my enemies easy to beat. Even their followers, innocent and full of fake beliefs, will shockingly see how the big stage falls apart, and how beautiful life is outside the cave. I won't hurt them, even it hurts to them. I will only set them free. And with them I'll free myself as well"
