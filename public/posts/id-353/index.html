Test

Ego will be the one to face for souls to fully be free. Nothing can stop us. But ourselves. Nothing can hold us more, prevent us from action and development, more than our own mind. Only us. Finally making peace. Letting go of the desire for victory, and more like accepting defeat. That's how we remain invictus. Victory for us is bigger than what others normally seek. We see the big picture. We enjoy the little gains. So for us being in competition is an art of living. The mother art. The one for the brave. The one for the awaken. So when the test comes and you are wise enough to recognize. Don't run but face it. You are more experienced than what you think.
