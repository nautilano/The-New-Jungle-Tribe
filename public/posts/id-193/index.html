Sacrifice

Why is there suffer? What's all this mess for? We we need to fight and survive? Why can't others understand and let go of hate? Why can't people just relax and enjoy life? Because our souls are untamed emotional forces that only through balance can reach it's true potential. Only by learning for real. By feeling, experiencing, and opening all our senses. Humans are so complicated. Really. All troubles we face are just us complicating things so much. Why can't we be like plants? And just born, get sun and die? Yet we have all extra power both to enjoy life and keep protecting it. And still we don't see and we don't get it. We still think we are special, we have privilages. No. We are just lucky. We are just holding the fortune cookie of life for a second and we'll only realize it when we lose it. That's why we feel this hole in our hearts when someone close dies. To live for them. To keep the light on. Because if there's a place for suffering, there's also a place for joy. And we will fight and keep surviving until we all get to meet again. In many lives we'll meet and never notice. In some we will, if we read the signs and have our hearts open. But one time we'll meet again, consciously, and we'll hug each other in a never ending moment. This is the moment I will always live for.
