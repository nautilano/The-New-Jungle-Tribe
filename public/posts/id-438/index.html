Womens voice

Women and men meeting or crashing is already a magic episode on its own. Before this meeting ever happens there was a concept of both. The impossible, the dream. And then it happens, and how different it was. As coming from different planets, realities or realms. Men are built with so much wounds, so much imperfection. Designed to battle and endurance. And women are all of that and much more. Hence that only disadvantage. To be good at all and thinking they are not. So yes, our energy core. It starts from the center of our bellys. And men struggle with that a lot. Uncontrol and self sabotage of our souls. Our voice is a sign of that. Whatever it comes from our throats traces back history of pain and love. And then women comes. With a similar design but different reaction. They may be hurt but never lost. The woman voice never really gets silenced even if they try to. It's just mental. Men, on the other hand, don't have that immune power as women do. We are destroyed and shattered in pieces and only a Goddess can fully heal us. That is women for us. We all pursue them because that’s what everybody does. But only a few really know why.
