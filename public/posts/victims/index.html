Victims

"I see now we are all victims of life. Why hate? There's only compassion now. I see you as a kid. As the one dumb kid who once was me. I see the fear, the empty eyes. You haven't seen, you haven't noticed yet. Not your fault, so why hate? Not at all. Not in this case. I choose to open and sacrifice, I choose to end this chain of lies. If not, then when? You may see and maybe you don't even care. Still your soul has memory, and even if ego is there claiming his space, one day in your dreams, one day in silence or in darkness, you will remember, and you will know what to do. At that moment you will see and reality will change. You are now in the big team playing in the great leagues. You will see it wasn't too hard. Just waking up. After that there's no more internal fight, just peace in your soul and mind. After that you will see who's the real boss and why we are not just fighting, more than that, we are just vibing. We just follow the natural law of life. We are just protecting, not with innocence but with knowledge. With feeling, emotion and sacred calling. You are now in the train of life, inviting everyone on track. You may fall, you may cramble, but the light will always guide you. Once a victim, now a keeper."
