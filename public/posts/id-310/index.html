One time

“Everything  is happening one time. That's one extra reason to defend it. You see the all those faces, all those stranded souls? Some of them don't notice, some don't have a clue, but every moment they have is a chance to redeem. No other time will be the same. And no matter how bad the situation is, you cannot run but only learn from it. So is either this, or so beautiful that you try so much to repeat it. So much to find a way to have the same conditions, the same feeling and outcome. Try no more otherwise you lose your shots. No situation is the same, that's the beauty and magic of the moment. That's what the accident of love is about, the beautiful science of randomness. And then it's us. Having the power of consciousness, the magic box of memories that just gets bigger once you wake up. The past no longer hunts you, but it's visited by you willingly. It's you and all your previous versions working for the same goal. No longer you need to fight. No longer you have to worry about letting go of the rope. It’s you giving the hand to the past for the future there will be. A future build from peace.”
