Eternal lose

"I know no matter what I say…she won't believe me. If she's Gaia herself, I have no chance, no option left. Already tried and failed. One time is enough to be stabbed. So next time I need to be wiser. To love her so much like no one. The kind of love she can't comprehend because she never felt. She can't reject because she won't see it coming. And in the end I won't have her. But she'll have herself. She'll be awake and see she won't need no more. No more running. No more places to scape. For her to see her power, I need to sacrifice myself. What others can't take. What others never thought. It only takes a stranded soul full in love. "
