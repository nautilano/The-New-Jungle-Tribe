Action

"Any medicine will give you the knowledge, confidence and natural calm that you are close to the truth. But it won't make any move for you. So take the plunge and play some chess. I'm the one influencing minds but I'm not the only one. I do what I can. I can blow the air and make appear a star. I can also make the weather warm and more romantic or melancholic. I can even make a bird whistle a melody so the DJ hears and put the right song at the party. I can paint the most beautiful sunset and choose the right wine for you. But I can't make them see or feel. That's on you. That's your job. You see the door, you invite them. I make the party, you dance with them."
