Runaways

What better toul can the jerk use but an enemy inside ourselves? The last final boss we need to beat to finish the game. Our ego, a big concentration of fears built along many years, many lives. Trapping us, preventing us to fully live. Yet fear now is accepted, and even comprehended. We tend to be soft with it, give it a chance to grow. Only to be betrayed in the most subtle and dishonored way. Being lied. How can we fight it if it's all darkness? How can we have a chance if we don't don't know where to go? There's school for everything, but there's no school for facing fears. Wrong. The school of fear is this one right now. Real life. The test is the dream, us fighting other demons while the one to beat is inside our minds drinking some tea. You have to know. There's no other way around. Full and plenty of joy comes once you wake up. No matter how beautiful the dream you are, you'd better wake up first or see it vanish piece by piece. It's a slow painfully watch that you wouldn't like to repeat. But the thing is, those who are sleeping tend to dream more and more, so only you have to power to finish this cycle and move on. Only you have the chance to run away from the big illusion and see life the way it really is. That's how true balance will be finally achieved.
