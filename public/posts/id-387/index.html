Healing

Fully enjoying a genuine moment is a sign of healing. The past is not holding you, not bringing you back. But more like enjoying with you. We always get better, we never get worse. We just get pushed back by ego to in order for it to survive. Hidden in little dark reflections, with  so much fear of being forgotten, of being left behind. Once we are finally at peace we feel our head is lighter. Our sight is now more focused and even more details and colors are shown. Things we didn’t see before, or things we tried to run from. We feel now the energy all throughout the body. We want to hug, kiss and dance. But we do it consciously. We do it as we know the importance. If we don’t, who will? We are all on the same team. Now as witnesses everything is so clear. All our duties, our tasks. We all share the same responsibility. We all lose. We all win. We all share the same joy. The same feelings. That’s the way to keep it.
