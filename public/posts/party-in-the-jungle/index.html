Party in the jungle

There is a moment in life for everything. Moments to laugh, moments for crying. Moments to learn, moments for teaching. Time for talking, time to be silent. There are special moments, and they are sacred ones. Now, when we are standing in front of Gaia. When we are in this precious moment, so important, we should be grateful and feel proudly for just being there. Life was so good to us and we were skilled enough to reach this moment of triumph. Because it is. The time has come for you to be fully conscious and open to recieving the medicine of life. You enter now, not to the classroom. She's not just a simple teacher. She's the principal. And don't worry, you won't be scolded in front of the other students. No, first of all this is a private meeting, and all the other elements are very respectful, so they are all busy performing their tasks. Second, she's a good principal. She knows you very well. That's why she called you and that's why she's there, present for you, ready to listen to each other. Don't feel shy or embarrassed for the past, bad feelings or thoughts. She knows everything already, and still she allowed you to find your way to her. But you need to be in good condition, good state of mind and soul, otherwise she will act as a mirror and won't be able to do all her job. She doesn't want you to feel uncomfortable. She wants you to trust, let go, and open your heart to all the love and comprehension of everything we are. We are all together in this, for you to understand you need to let your body and soul feel the unity, the creation, the party. There are moments to dance and sing, there are moments to talk and listen. Now, if what you seek is answers and healing, then it's time to be silent, listen and feel.
