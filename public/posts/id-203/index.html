Limits

There's no limit with Ayahuasca. You cannot encapsulate it's power, you cannot impose any condition. The dieta, the rules, it's all set for you to be afraid and not embrace your full potential, hence the medicine won't entirely do it's work on you. What defines the "work"? You may ask, since we are all so different, right?. Well, yes, but there is only one truth. And this is not following anyone but your own gut. Only when you know any of these rules apply and how asleep you were, is when you are fully indepentent, and no longer live in automatic mode. It's only in this scenario where the medicine can do it's work on you. The way it's supposed to be. The medicine is strong and wants you to be strong. It doesn't want you to be weak and have so many blockages in your mind and heart. It wants you to understand your full potential and how you are capable of anything. There's no roof for love and light. Already being in a bottle is too much for the medicine. You and the medicine are supposed to set each other free. And that only works when you believe entirely in you. Believing is already jumping from the cliff. After that you need to trust, the medicine and the jungle will do all the work. You just need to jump. This is not just you being one more client of a big company. This is you facing the giant monster you were supposed to face in order to level up in the game. And you can do this, in your own terms. Nobody else will do it for you. And nobody can impose anything on you. All you have to do is wait for the given chance. As long you remain open you'll be wise enough to recognize.
