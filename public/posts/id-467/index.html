Religion

When you try to understand the problems of the world through the main concepts of any religion, you would find yourself having a tough time. But under Gaia’s rule, everything suddenly makes sense. It’s just very simple. We were just looking a different way and look where it took us. Now it is time to stop messing around and start doing things right. Nature is to be respected and praised, otherwise we pay. Sometimes we’ll even pay for others, so be ready for what’s coming. Have you ever been loved by a woman and then see that love turn into hate? Have you seen how that angel can turn into the devil? Have you hurt her so much that she would practically forget about your existence and do things that you never imagined she could do? Have you love her so much and lost her to realize she was the only good thing that ever happened to you in this mad world? Yes. Only once you understand that you will see that any religion is just for kids. And real men are actually in bed making babies, being a good father, good husband, a good friend. The only religion there is, is to make a better place for them to be. Without them, nothing is left. So do what it takes and don’t complain.
