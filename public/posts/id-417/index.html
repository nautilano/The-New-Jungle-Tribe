Magic beings

Every myth and every story of humankind comes from the truth. Sometimes though, it gets distorted according to some interests. So we need to be wise and differentiate the fake from the truth. So yes, all those magic beings you heard. They were true at some point. How to really know though? Well. It’s all hard until you become one of them. Then the picture opens and you finally understand. In the Ayahuasca escenario, particularly. You have the patients and the shamans talking about demons and mysterious beings from the Amazonian lore. What they never say is that people become those beings during the ceremony. You have the magic princess. You have the demon tricksters. The lone warrior. The naïve student. The soul hunters and shapeshifters. You have literally every being of every story at every moment in life. You just need to identify. What magic being you will be? Luckily, once you wake up and start to heal. You can choose and be who you want to be. It’s always you, it’s always being yourself. But once a magic being, you get some more duties to fulfill.
