Comedy of life

Laughter is a gun. The kind of gun you want to keep on hand. If you lose it. You may lose your life without the need to die. That’s worse, way worse than you can imagine. To be dead while alive. Every dream becomes a nightmare while on that. So this is more than just an advice. I pray for the sake of your soul that you never lose your laughter. More than an action, more than a feeling. It is way of living. If the show is a joke. By laughing you never lose  your track. You keep going. No matter how many ups and downs or wounded you end up. Gods may take your life, but never your laugh. So in a way you are invincible. Undefeatable. Yes, you are a victim, as we all are. But you are also in control all the time. If you control yourself, you control everything else. So yes, you will be mad and sad sometimes. But if you open your heart and mind and try to see more than what it seems, you’ll see there was always a reason to laugh.
