Jungle

"Do you know why I chose the jungle?. I didn't choose the jungle. The jungle is where I was born. A raw, hard and intense environment. Perfect place for a perfect accident. What could be more ideal? Beautiful and reluctant at the same time. A place that combines fire and mist, storm and sunsets. So much life, everywhere, every second, at your ears, at your shoulders, everywhere claiming what's theirs. Here the cycle of life happens nonstop. It's a party, a celebration. We are all alive and we are all dying. We are not afraid. Why would we? Here we are all friends. We all understand each other. We know our parts to play, the importance of our talents. Here there is no mistery, no secret to unveil. Here death is not reluctant. More like embraced. Here we welcome change, transformation. Since we all know what this show is about. Life, with all the colors, all sounds, all nuances. This is the real treasure. This is why you all came here. Not for gold. Why is it gold for if there's no life? This is to enjoy and to protect. To be part of. Of course some may forget, that's why I chose my soldiers. To keep the legacy on. For them to be an example, for them to keep the fire on. In exchange you will enjoy life like no one else. But there's a catch. You need to fulfill your duties. Otherwise I take it all from you. But this is not the worst part. I chose you for a reason. So nothing is worse than you feeling miserable for failing your mission”
