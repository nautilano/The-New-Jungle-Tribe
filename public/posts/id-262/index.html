Changa club

When you start in the world of psychedelics there's basically one thing in mind. Visions. To see things we never saw before. How funny, right?. As if dreams were not enough, and reality is so boring that we want so much to see something new. And then we start judging psychedelic experiences in terms of how crazy the visuals were. It happens to Ayahuasca, LSD, and even San Pedro. So unfair. It's like judging a sports teacher when delivering a class of linguistics. No wonder why the plant teachers close the doors, because they realize some students are lost. But the medicine is so great, and love is so immense, that there had to be a teacher who would give people exactly what they want. Changa. Since the Source needs people to be fully awaken, the teachers should be careful at showing extra visuals to the students. Because this would rather make them pursuit more visions, more dreams to switch instead of facing reality. But Changa came the same way as LSD, his fellow partner. With a little help of Merlins, it came as an accident (not really, it’s just science) As if breaking dimensions is not enough, changa and Merlins choose their students meticulously. And they never fail, no matter what appears. Every action they do has a reason and the students end up being of great use at the end. You see, Changa actually goes beyond visions. Connecting directly to our pineal gland, same as Cannabis with our cannabinoids, creating a direct pathway to the source that breaks any limit of space-time. It's like the train of love, but without the train. So you are actually flying or dividing your soul everywhere at any time. So what you see is not a vision, it's real. And as in movies, there's no limit at all. If you see something ridiculous, that's your ridiculous world. In Changa you are in the source itself, with all the power of creation granting you full passage. With you being not just the observer but the painter himself. For the ones wanting to trip so hard that they experience a visual breakthrough, Changa is the way to go. Still, this is not so easy to be found. If you are crazy enough you may be able to find Changa and Merlin in your same path. And if you are lucky and wise, you may soon realize visuals are just the funny part. The beginner mode of the game, the one some prefer and play until the end.
