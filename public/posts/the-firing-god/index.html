The firing God

Every-time you tune to the Sun for a minute, and then open your eyes, everything has a different color.  The same with every medicine. You may not notice instantly, but life has changed it's tonality. You see more colors, you feel more balanced. The flower inside you has received light, now is on you to make it bloom. It's on you to be conscious about it. When you believe and you are honest with your intentions, the Sun may give you the answers you need. How? You may ask. Well, the answers are already inside. A big ocean of ideas waiting for some light to come in. To start boiling, to start cooking up. The dreams have made you forget about it. The sun will help you. It will remind you the exact moment of the explosion of life. That intensity and heat that only comes from love. That fire that allowed everything to exist. But you need to let it in. The firing God is always there. But it won't force anything. It doesn't wanna burn you out. It's you, the one in charge, the children of the creation. So you need to wake up and remember all tools are out there for you to work with them and continue the legacy of love.  Sometimes the dream you are may be so strong, that even Helios himself won't be able to do much. You know, he cannot even light up the night by himself. He needs the Moon for that. So, if you are a good soldier who is struggling to find your way out of your nightmares, he would send a soldier of himself. A fierce and freedom warrior. A woman. Yes, if Gaia recruits boys. Helios recruits girls. What did you expect? At the end, it's all the same. Anyways, the ones Helios work with are not to take lightly. You see Gaia can make mistakes. Boys are stupid, and that's not her fault. But women develop faster, so it only takes a few lives for them to understand and play the game smarter. These warriors represent the most mysterious aspects of Gaia. The one no one can explain or define. There's only one time in life of every wordsmith where the action of naming something stays at nothing. That's when they meet these women. These flowers of life carry the entire force of Helios canalyzed in their bodies. When to use it? Only they know. Sometimes they are conscious of their power, sometimes they are not. But I think they always are. Their whole existence is a message itself. You cannot go past them without getting a sunburn. They do what they want. And Helios pretty much knows that. So if a good soldier is being freed by these breathtaking warriors, that's actually a miracle and reason to celebrate. You see, at the end, Helios and Gaia need their warriors to help each other, otherwise the puzzle never gets solved.
