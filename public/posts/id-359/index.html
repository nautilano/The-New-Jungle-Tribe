Crazyness

Crazy is the one who tries to explain. You need to accept it. They can't understand if they haven't been there. Only them, opening the doors with their own keys, that's how they really learn. But before that save yourself some time otherwise you'll lose your mind. They know that state, so they will know the moment when you are slowly getting there. They will see you in the same field and think this is familiar, this must be the right place. They will think they are right but they are only wrong. Feeding their ego with little confirmations of a fake illusion. That's the collective dream. Trapping people as much as they can, making the ones awaken to doubt, to have internal battles and fall on their side. But the truth is only one. They are in different fake realities but there's only one for you to be.  With balance and information you'll find strength to remain sane in this game. So trust, trust until the end. Others go crazy because they went to the edge. You see their eyes can see trough walls, but they prefer to look away. Not you though. You have learned and you have accepted. You don't run from the truth. You let the light in and now you handle the switch. You know and you differentiate. You know when to talk, when to observe. When to look back and when to look ahead.
