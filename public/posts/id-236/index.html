Bravery

If you love now, you will be loving more. You will love consciously, and your love will be stronger. Otherwise the dream will end at some point, no matter how hard you try to keep sleeping. The dream will end and you'll stay shocked at your bed, without knowing what's next. But in the source, while in the train of love, everything is clear. Nothing to be afraid. You will see there's just so much to share, and so much to do and protect. If you hate, you are dumb, and you will see how dumb you were. In that case, yes, take as much as you want. And one day you'll tune in and see the vast doors of light, but only for a limited short of time. For you to come back you'll need to let go of weight. And that is you breaking your own self. For the ego to run away because there's no more space. It's you, only you now, and the medicine awaits.
