The cage we are all in

The cage. Yes, the world we live in. Call it the matrix, the dream. So many names. They are all the same. But there is one we all forget. The one that is actually more important than everything else. This is not the cage we should break from. At least not consciously or willingly. The time will come for that. This cage is actually the one we should take care of. Protect, accept and love. Only through that process we can let all the love and light flow in order to live plenty and fully and make the world the best place for us and everyone. This is cage is our body. We don't try to get free from it. We try to live with it. It's our most important resource and the one we are supposed deal with until the end of our days. We are not supposed to get worst. We are not supposed to rot. We live and we evolve. We are shapeshifters. We just don't know until we start. Then you see there's no limit. It was not a cage after all. We are victims of life but our body is supposed to be our most valuable gun. Our most trustworthy ally. We. Ourselves. Our mind, heart, spirit. Don't be afraid to unlock the power. Don't limit yourself. Let the love flow and recognize what it wants. To be loved and to be noticed. To be treated as the most important matter in the world. You are more than body. You are more than just a soul. You are everything that love was supposed to be.
