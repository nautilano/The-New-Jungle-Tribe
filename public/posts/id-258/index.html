Ayahuasca Feeling

I don't need to ask you "Are you healed?" Because I already know the answer. How can you get the full power of love from Ayahuasca if you were never in the right environment? The one with the party of life. The one you hear every being in the jungle playing psychedelic cumbia. How can you be fully in tuned with the source if you always had so many ideas and extra concepts clouding your mind and weighing up your soul?. How can you fully embrace the infinte love and light if you never let go of fears, and made peace with your inner child? One mistake we always tend to accept is saying "Ayahuasca is different for everyone". Yes, and No. Everyone is having a different journey, but the path is the same, the one we all get to cooperate between each other. Is the same path we all get to help, forgive, love, and learn to let go. But Ayahuasca is reconnection, acceptance, awareness, insertion of love, wisdom and comfidence. And this should be the same for everyone. Ayahuasca is no hate, no nightmares, no bad experiences, otherwise is not done the right way. Of course people see demons, because they are surrounded by them. With so much extra elements of no use. Making people have more visions, instead of them opening their eyes.  Ayahuasca is not a prescribed doctor. It's going to make you aware of your body only if you tune in. With movement and massages. And then give you the peace you need to accept. We can all enjoy and make the best of what we have. Running from it and fighting it will only distract us from the joy of life. Death is part of life. But who does really mean it?. Only a few. But this is Ayahuasca healing. Accepting death and not being afraid. This is not us jumping from cliffs and making any stupid decision. This is us living plenty and fully, with so much peace that we have the comfidence to see death straight in the eyes when we have to. Honor. If we live a life of honor we may even trick death. New doors and new tricks open up for the ones who know how to play. This is Ayahuasca. Only in this canvas is where the painting can be done.
