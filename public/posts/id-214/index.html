Master

The true master doesn't love everyone, and he's not willing to give his teachings to any person, not even with all the money of the world. This a truth nobody wants to accept. Yet, we have a whole industry producing new "masters" through a very "juicy" initiation. The true master is tired of so much dumbness in the world, so he or she are actually enjoying their time, away from the reflectors, away from the trouble. They know this is a world for the noisy, so they better enjoy the silence while they can. They know at some point the right student will come, someone who already went through all the bullshit on its own. Only at that moment the talk and patience will be enjoyed. When they speak the same language, in the same frequency of autonomy. Only the true awaken can see the unbalance of the world. And how there's no one to believe but our own. Only us, only ourselves can master our soul and mind. This is what the plants have been trying to tell us all this time. And this is what the others don't want us to know.
