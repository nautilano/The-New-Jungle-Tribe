The universal hammock

Wake up, go out and see you are not in a fight. You are in a truce. Not everyone is lucky enough. While some are in constant war, you can lay down in the universal hammock of life. No matter how you have it. Whether from this life or another. You have earned it somehow. There’s no coincidence in this matter. So be proud and enjoy it. Things will change anyway. So make the best of it while it lasts.
