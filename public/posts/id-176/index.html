Breaking reality

Medicine always finds it's way. No matter what you do. No matter how much make up you put on it. How many lies you believe, or how many dreams you are on. It will come at some point, whether in this live or another, but it will come. So yes, you have "the call", you have the curiosity. But after this you need to understand where you are standing on. What are the things that truly make sense? Is this magic? Is this science? Do you really believe in love? Back to the source. Back to the simple. Remember, nothing got away from the explosion of love, and love always expands. So basically every little thing in the universe has the same capacity, the same potential and power of a star. That's why plants are medicine. Mushrooms and animals. They can all teach us the most crutial aspects of the creation. From that is where it continues and never stops. Women themselves are a psychedelic experience. Posibly the strongest of them all, cause they are all Gaia, they are all guardians of the source. Even without noticing, even without being aware. Lucky for us, art is in the viewers eye. Everything is psychedelic. We just need to wake up and notice. Feel the the waves, the energy, the vibes, the dizziness of life.
