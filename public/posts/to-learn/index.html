To learn

You only swim in waters. That's the only way to learn. Jump and see, you will realize there was no reason to be afraid. Yes, thought water can be crazy, with Gaia you will be safe. Yes, she can tolerate you being nervous, but don't think she will stay for long with that vibration. Still, she understands, and wishes you the best. The right time will come for you to understand. There's fear, always. But there's love always too. What do you choose? Jump, the train is coming. But no, it won't run you over. It has a special designed spot for you to fall since you are the main character. Expecting you, knowing your conditions. Knowing your weight, your height, even your special plate for after. Because you will be rewarded. An inner deep peace flowing throughout your body, welcoming you and making you feel comfortable. Like a child getting a cake of a party he was not invited, only to realize it was a surprise party. All for you, everyone clapping. Everyone there holding the same flame. A celebration for the champion. It's all because you made up your mind and chose to learn. No one was hurt but the ego, who is now away knowing his boundaries. You see it wasn't hard? It was just you taking a hop. The hop of Gaia. The hop of life.
