Simplicity

Life works in a mysterious set of patterns that no one can fully understand. The little accident of love has grown up to develop it's own intelligence, making the right circumstances to happen to achieve it's purpose. Yes, our minds have a play on this, but our minds can also be influenced easily. Therefore, life will happen in the less expected situations and make sure the good outcome is developed at the end. Nobody else has a saying in this. Not even Gaia, not even Helios, much less the Jerk. The only sure thing is the beginning and the end. But the journey is a mistery. How much we lose, and how much we win. There's a plant that represents this exact mistery of life. A white, bell shaped flower, showing its shyness and humbleness on direction to earth. You don't need to drink the Toé to get her teachings. The Toé comes and finds you on her own particular ways. In your dreams and in real life. Yes, the Toé is the power of life itself working to influence you and the actions around you. It's like an antenna, a sign, designed to canalized the full power of love to protect you. And it will follow you everywhere you go, in one way or another. To think you can mix it with Ayahuasca to have more intense visions and dreams is a complete mistake many shamans do. Even drinking by itself is risky. The Toé doesn't want you to keep dreaming or believing in lies. She wants you fully conscious living in real life. She wants you to wake up and get action. She's already doing everything to push you and working on it's own existence to show up to you, and start the linking process. All you have to do is trust, and she will show up to you as the most beautiful mistery of life.
