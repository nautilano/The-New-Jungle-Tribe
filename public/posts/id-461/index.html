The burning inside

This is way simpler than any poetic words can explain. The burning inside. The one we feel everyday when we wake up. Some of us are just not aware of it, and we get misuse of the fire. But how to do it then? Is it something you have to repeat yourself and get it from magic? No. This comes from the deepest and purest will of your being that only comes with acceptance. Not just a happy one, sadly. Most of the time this fire comes with pain and resignation. To live to fight another day. To be alive when you want to be dead. This takes courage and guts that only comes once you’ve been in hell. This is it. Everyday we make a deal. To live without fear and give our best. No other choice but to finish it until the end.
