Hidden Kingdom

“As I was walking around the trees in darkness. With only the moon and fireflies guiding. I felt an invitation. A magic calm sensation as natural as breathing comes. There’s no fear and no strange feeling of unknown. It’s just attraction and safety relief. I see the river and the moonlight beams reflecting on it. Some boats, some birds doing their thing. What a beauty landscape that is. I approach and then I feel betrayed. Some flies coming taking my blood. I said what’s going on? If we are all friends. Why can’t I just be there and appreciate? But now I see and understand they were only doing their job. By having my blood they were testing. Recognizing my anatomical code as being part of the source. Here you are, they must say. Yes, here I am. But enough biting for me as I’m not supposed to be here. That’s the real message and goal of the flies. Yes you can be in land and enjoy being there, but in the river and the sea you need to pay some fees. Even in Ayahuasca, even in the middle of million realms. You need to stick to the physical world. And in the depths of the river, particularly the Amazon. There’s life and there’s something there so magical to the protect. Nothing less but the mysteries of planet Earth itself”
