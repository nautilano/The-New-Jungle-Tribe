Dogma

You have to remain open when you jump into the spectrum of the medicine. Otherwise you'll trip yourself over and end up defending barriers of ego. The medicine is not you believing in desperating attempts of the system to survive. It wants you to fully see the picture for how it is. With no limit and no made up encapsulation. It wants you to fully claim sovereignty of your thoughts. No more giving space to unwanted ideas that don't align to the inner self. This is you. Your journey. Know the importance of this. And how everyone else is asleep, trying so much do defend a collapsed dream. So be careful and trust only your guts and experience. You are the hero, so take full responsibility of this.
