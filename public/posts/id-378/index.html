Explanations

We try to explain magical expressions and we end up complicating ourselves way more. Yes, it's good to understand, but some things are made to appreciate. Specially when the joy of life has been forgotten, and the connection to the source has been interrupted. That's it. That's the answer you seek. What you thought as a kid, what you used to believe. It’s all there inside. You just got yourself lost in the strange ways of life. This is what we all go through. This is the illusion to break for living consciously. This is us finally making peace with our true self, the inner kid and the mature adult. Nothing else. No ego to mask from. No lies to go around of. Only plain truth accepting and inner self acknowledging. Yes, it may be hard at first. But it’s beautiful after. And tha’s how it remains.
