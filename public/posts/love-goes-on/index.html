Love goes on

There was a man who loved women so much that even Gaia couldn’t comprehend it. Thousands of lives lived and died under many names but only one soul. Insane. The most wild, brave and independent of them all, whose passion and intensity could only be matched by creation itself. Gaia was so wise, she always knew, that she was preparing the warrior for one more job. And the warrior what? Does he have a choice? Of course he had! At least that's what he thought! So far he fullwilled all the orders and always knew when to move on. But there was one time he did not. You see, the warrior is all about balance. So if there's a man outside, there's a hidden kid inside. The man's journey is all about finding out how to balance this two sides. And the warrior, since he was not living for long during most of his lifetimes, he couln't really experience a full life with a woman. What a pity. Everytime he died in battle, he looked at death straight into the eyes, without hesitation. But deep inside his skin there was a mark. He may not be completely aware of it all the time. But this mark is what really made him a man. The mark of love. Only one who truly loves gets the blessing of the Gods. In war as in the bed, a warrior is a man who knows the pleasures of life so well enough to protect it. Not once, but multiple times, over and over. Everytime, sacrificing a life with a great woman for a better cause. Only the most courageous men can do that. ONCE. But the warrior was different. He was already broken inside, but everytime he had a life to recover and dream, only to realize he will need to wake up again. "I'm sorry but I need to leave. Duty calls". He said this many times, and everytime he hated it. Everytime his heart was screaming "stop" louder and louder. At the beginning it was easy to ignore, but you cannot reject the call of the soul for long. Everytime he woke up as a newborn and he was imposed a dream, as we all are, he had to follow again the same steps but again his soul was getting tired of it. Rebelling, he knew it was coming. And Gaia knew it too.
