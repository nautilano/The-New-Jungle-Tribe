The old dreamer

"Once again I see myself in the mirror of life. In the eyes of this old man, I recognize another victim, another lost in the path. To think he's spent all his life dreaming, and we both fall for the same lies. How tricky and how sad. You see now how serious is this fight. Nobody is wise enough. We all get caught and enchanted by the same illusion, the same desires. Our experience can only get us to a point where we know too much, only to realize how miserable we are. Dreams can last years, even take a whole life. Only lucky ones and the brave get really awaken. Only true miracles of life, make people win the lottery of reality. No other way can you be granted the unveilment of eyes, only you and the blessing of randomness, the same cause of the accident of life."
