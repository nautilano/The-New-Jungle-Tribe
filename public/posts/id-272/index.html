Encapsulation

You see, the thing with hate is that it spreads like a virus. The only one sickness we should be healed from. The biggest true demon we have to face. On his account there has been wars, disasters and distortions. A waste of life in the most disgracious ways. But what is there, then? If not a way of solving the case. Ayahuasca. Acting as insertion of love. Reminding that you never stopped loving, and you never stopped being loved. That we are all part of the explosion of life and we have all been affected by the accident of love. Nothing got away and no one left behind. Once you accept that you realize there's no one to hate, no one to look down. We all deserve love no matter what. No condition and no exception to discuss. Ayahuasca is magical and can literally do anything if it has to. And if hate is on the way for a soul to be freed, it will always find a way to vanish it. Of course, ego will attach and prevent it from leaving, unchaining a fierce battle between soul and mind that will only bring good things at the end. This are the kind of battles Ayahuasca specializes. She leaves the visions and sensorial pleasures for others. She remains humble, since she can do anything. Even breaking space-time, or causing holes in eternity.
