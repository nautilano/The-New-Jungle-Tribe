Words

How was Ayahuasca? People tend to ask. To the brave souls who answer the call. And then we talk about the vision. A mistake in which we all fall. To talk about it yes, just for the fun. But to explain after seeing it all, why care? If they won't understand. What we see is nothing more than one more channel. One more tv show of our own. When the medicine unfolds the curtains, giving you a free pass to see it all. That's you though. You are here because of your choices. For othes to understand, they will have to break their heads. So don't do yours. The best to say is look at them. You can feel compassion, you can share it too. They need that, a big expression of love, not just a small piece. Don't make others dream. They can do it on their own. Don't make others look for distractions instead of the great goal. The same we all go, the same we all come from. So words are small in the big picture. Love can't be spoken but being shown. Ayahuasca is more than we see, way more than we all think. With the current worldwide distortion is hard to explain the true things. So how was Ayahuasca?, you ask me? I smile and say "What do you think?".
