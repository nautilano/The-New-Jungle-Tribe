Independence day

This is the day of independence day. The day for you to reclaim full autonomy of your existence. The day for you to celebrate your past, present and future. No more regrets or internal fights. You now see it all from the center, the universal chair. You see it now from the perspective of a master. Hence you understand why they chose you and how important was your role all along. Now you see you would have cheered yourself all the time. You would have helped yourself and gave a hand to the innocent past. But also you would have let yourself learn and understand. To give yourself the confidence that you would figure things out. Others couldn’t take it but you can. That’s the strength you earned and now it’s your gun. To defend against demons and demonstrations of the ego. For you to keep sane and balanced in the spectrum of life, and to help others keep themselves on track. Party and celebrate. Welcome to the full version of life.
