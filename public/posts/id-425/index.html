Sleep realm

The sleep realm is calling you. A place of truce and pause. A place where you depart from being yourself to becoming nothing at all. Sure thing reality is the best, but as it is heaven, it can be hell as well. So rest, dear warrior. To enjoy the sleep you need to be fully in. I know it’s hard though, as your body, soul and mind already had so much despair. We don’t leave reality without traces of it. There is information and experiences in every cell. But if we are lucky and find out the true balance you may fully rest to wake up once again. And then sleeping becomes a friend. The one that you learn to appreciate and take care. Sleep and dream and keep moving on. Learning to play is knowing when to stop. Make dreams a planning board where for a moment you forget the limits and rules that your conscious head imposes. So rest, dear child. There’s a brand new playground waiting for you wake up. And it needs your renewed soul and heart.
