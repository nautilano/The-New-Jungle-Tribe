Elements

Everything you see is an expression of love. And how is love made for? You know that answer. There has to be intention, passion, intensity. The clash of mountains, the fall of trees. There is no creation that holds still. There is always movement, change. After the thunderbolt has hit and the fire is ignited, there's no turning back. Transformation happens and the dance of time starts its number. When Gaia made love and realized her true power, her light expanded and covered everything that was in darkness. She brought the colors, the taste, the feelings. The knowledge and the source, with the strongest force of all, got inserted in all living things there were. No longer the world was unloved. No longer darkness would rule without resistance. The world turned into Gaia's garden, and she knew of every plant and every flower. But how to protect? She doubted, any accident can happen. How you make sure the plants still get planted, or their knowledge be well used. The plants only know of love, but don't count on falling on the wrong. Their plants know their duty and what good are they for. But do we? She later realized humans are not to be trusted. So she trusted the other ones. All other beings that don't complicate too much themselves. The party should never stop. Otherwise you never know if it will ever come back. She didn't want to take any risk and decided to do her best to protect her creation, her little accident. All animals in the jungle will have only one sacred duty. To never stop singing. No matter how much noise, interruption or destruction they face. They will keep on with their music. Making it even louder and gaining any terrain they can. What better way to be tuned than listen to the ones already on it? This is what the plants are for, specially the psychedelics. They are tuners, tools that would help us go back in track. Go back into the party of life. The invitation is there, because the party is always there. You just have to notice and see, and realize that waking up is not just a chance you have, but a choice always on your hands.
