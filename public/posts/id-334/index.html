What you get

“I gave you a lot and you didn’t appreciate. Now I won’t give you anything. I hope you learn. I hope you realize. And more importantly. I hope you are well. Now I move on. As you did too. But you see, even if you are gone. You will still enjoy. Maybe you are not conscious, maybe you will forget the show. But still the world includes you. And I work for the world. I’m always working. Just not the way you do. I am flowing. I am celebrated and honored by my souldiers. They have me. In them. And they know how to be. How to live and die. How to be useful. How to be part of the great picture. How to take what you get and make something of it. Yes. They all help me. And I help them. Like this. That’s how the world keeps. How life keeps happening. And how hope will never end. We’ll never succumb to it. We will always live and find a way. Because we don’t forget. We never close the doors. We open the windows. We let artists of life come and join the party. We teach and we give roof to stranded ones coming for joy and light. Yes. I move on. But you will come with me. Just at your own pace.”
