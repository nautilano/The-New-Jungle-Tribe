Paradox

How do you miss a soldier and waste his warrior potential? Lossing him into the rebel paradox. The one final trick that keeps the Jerk winning. He realized we all have this natural feeling of rebelion, blissfully given by the strongest power of the source. For all to reach the light, for all to be free. Yet he's found a way to fill that empty space with vague rewards of ego. Accompanied by hate and fear, this will only continue and never improve things. There's no hate if there's understanding. Yet we live in a world lack of acceptance. We blindly believe in good, without even seeing the bad. That's us running away from the truth. So all blind warriors will fight for no reason, for no basis. Just following the path of anger instead of doing nothing. What a way to keep them entertained and keep oneself safe. The jerk would say.
