Spiritual paradox

People looking for spiritual wisdom normally end up in the other side of the road and even more lost than before. You know why? Because they would go anywhere just to run away from the truth. There can’t be good spirit without being grounded. Balance is the key. Sadly, people have followed the wrong examples of the extreme. You have “masters” demonizing and putting limits on the things that make us humans. Instead of accepting who we are with all the ingredients of the sauce. Humans can be useful and can be great, if only we start looking at ourselves instead of someone else. We need to stop judging and start understanding. But we won’t get to that if we keep running from the truth. To be fearless is actually easier to be said than done. We get that. The world is full of cowards and there is nothing wrong with that. This is where we all start. Even chickens are brave when there’s no option left. So don’t worry. Truth comes, even if it takes too long.
