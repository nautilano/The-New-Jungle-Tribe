Playing with demons

“I’ll play with fire and may get burned, but I will take the bad with me. And then only the good things will remain. At least enough to keep things in balance. I’m actually fire myself. But I’m more than that. I’m also water, wind and earth. So don’t worry about me, I will find another way to be. I mean who else can do this? But a demon himself. One that would break any rules of the game for a better cause. One that doesn’t have anything else to lose, other than ending up with his old self. That’s scarier than any army sent from hell. “
