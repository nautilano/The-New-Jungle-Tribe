"This is where I come to heal after facing worlds of dispair. A place of family, joy and rest. Here I can let my guard down and not be stabbed. Here I can be me and won't be judged. This is all the proof I need for the fight we are fighting. That nothing is in vain, and love will always prevail. It's for this. The kind of peace we all seek, but only a few can find. The lucky ones. The ones who tried enough to not lose themselves. The ones who were smart to act and wise to appreciate. The ones who knew when to stop and when to back up. The ones who said this is enough. This is where I lay down and rest. From this I only move with strong winds and rain. Only to make a better place for you and me, my dear."
