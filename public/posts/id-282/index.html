Filter

"You have to know, before being fully at peace, that this world is full of hate. Anywhere you go, anywhere you'll be. A truth most of us don't want to accept. That most of us will never change, no matter how much love and light you try to profess. Already thousands of years of getting everything wrong. Of course there are consecuences. But maybe this is how it should be. To think that everything can be perfect, without control or filter, is nothing less than naive. You can't defend love if you are too innocent. Otherwise you get lied and betrayed. I guess this is the constant fight of warriors. Always facing battles in the big spectrum of good and bad. And even with all the pain and understanding, the final act of letting go has to come. Not all can enter, not all can join the train of love. Acceptance is key. There's no healing or going forward without it. For those who attach and cling to the lost, nothing but good intentions remain. And love takes more than that."
